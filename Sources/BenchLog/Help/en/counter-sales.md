# Counter Sales

A counter sale sells parts or items without a repair: a charger, a case, a part someone repairs at home.

1. Open **Counter Sales** in the sidebar and click **New Counter Sale**, or choose **File > New Counter Sale…** (⌥⌘N) from anywhere.
2. Pick the customer, or leave it as a **walk-in**: the invoice then prints "Walk-in customer".
3. Add parts from stock, or type an item that is not in the inventory, with its price.
4. Issue the invoice. Parts from stock leave the shelf as *Sold*, in their stock history.

Every sale is on the **Counter Sales** page, newest first: search by customer, item, or invoice number, and select a sale to see its items, record the payment, open the PDF, or issue a credit note. A customer's own counter sales are also on their page.
