# Counter Sales

A counter sale sells parts or items without a repair: a charger, a case, a part someone repairs at home.

1. Choose **File > New Counter Sale…** (⌥⌘N), or the button on a customer.
2. Pick the customer, or leave it as a **walk-in**: the invoice then prints "Walk-in customer".
3. Add parts from stock, or type an item that is not in the inventory, with its price.
4. Issue the invoice. Parts from stock leave the shelf as *Sold*, in their stock history.

A customer's counter sales are on their page, with their repairs.
