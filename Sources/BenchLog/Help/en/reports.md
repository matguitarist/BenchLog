# Reports

The **Reports** page shows a month, a quarter, or a year:

- **Sales before taxes**: repair invoices, counter sales, and shop device sales, less credit notes.
- Each **tax** collected, ready for your return, and the **Total**.
- **Collected**: invoices paid, less credit notes refunded. **Owed**: invoices neither paid nor credited.
- **Inventory at cost** at the end of the period.
- Every invoice and credit note of the period, with its status. Right-click one to open its PDF, repair, or customer.

The arrows beside the period show the one before and after.

**Export CSV…** saves the period's documents for your accountant or a spreadsheet.
