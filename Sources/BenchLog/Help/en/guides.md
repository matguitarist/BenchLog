# Guides

A guide is a repair manual: a PDF, or a Markdown note such as an Obsidian vault page. It **covers** categories, brands, models, or revisions from the catalog, so a repair shows the guides for its device.

- **Add Guides…** on the Guides page adds files. Drop a folder to import many at once; BenchLog shows what each one covers before it adds them.
- A model's main guide (`_index.md` in a vault) is its **Overview** and comes first. A guide that covers nothing is **General**.
- **Open in Window** shows a guide in its own window, beside a repair.
- In a PDF, ⌘F finds text, ⌘G and ⇧⌘G go to the next and previous match, and ⌘+, ⌘−, ⌘0 zoom.
- Links between Markdown guides (`[[Note]]`) open the other guide.
