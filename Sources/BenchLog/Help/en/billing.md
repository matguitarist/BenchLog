# Estimates and Invoices

BenchLog prints estimates, invoices, and credit notes in French or English, with the taxes of your province or state.

## Estimates

**Repair > Print Estimate** makes a PDF from the repair's parts and labour. When the customer answers, choose **Approve** or **Refuse** on the repair. A repair waiting for an answer is *Awaiting Approval*.

## Invoices

**Repair > Invoice** (⇧⌘I) issues the next invoice, such as **F-0007**. An invoice is frozen: its number, lines, and taxes never change. When the customer pays, mark it **Paid** and choose how: cash, debit, credit, or e-Transfer. A Ready repair with no paid invoice shows under **To Collect**.

## Credit notes

To cancel an invoice, choose **Issue Credit Note…** on it. The credit note (**NC-0001**) reverses its lines and taxes. When you pay the customer back, mark it **Refunded**.

## Numbers

Invoice and credit note numbers follow each other with no gap. When the library is connected to a shop server, the numbers come from the server, so two Macs never use the same one. See [[sync|Sync and the Shop Server]].

> [!note]
> Taxes, registration numbers, and the hourly rate are in **Settings > Business**.
