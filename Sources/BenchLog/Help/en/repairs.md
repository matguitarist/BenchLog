# Repairs

A repair is one job on one device, from check-in to pickup. Each has a ticket number such as **#0042**.

## Check a device in

1. Choose **File > New Repair…** (⌘N). Pick the customer and the device, or add new ones.
2. Write the customer's **complaint**: what they say is wrong.
3. Choose the **approval rule**: *Go ahead*, *Ask for an estimate first*, or *Go ahead up to an amount* before taxes.
4. Enter the device's **passcode** if you need it. It stays hidden until you choose Show Passcode (⌥⌘P).
5. Take intake photos with **File > Import from iPhone or iPad**, or add photos and files (⇧⌘P).
6. Print the **intake slip** (⌘P) for the customer to sign.

## Status

A repair moves through **Intake, Diagnosing, Awaiting Approval, Waiting on Parts, In Progress, Ready**, and closes as **Picked Up** or **Cancelled**. Change it from the repair, or with **Repair > Status** (⌘1 to ⌘8). Each change goes on the repair's timeline.

## Notes and timeline

**Add Note** (⇧⌘K) adds a dated line: the diagnosis, a call to the customer, what you tried. The timeline shows notes and status changes in order.

## Parts and labour

- **Parts**: add a part from stock, or type one to order. A part is *Needed*, *Ordered*, *Received*, or *Installed*; installing takes it out of stock.
- **Labour**: by the hour at your hourly rate (Settings > Business), or at a flat price.

When the job is done, see [[billing|Estimates and Invoices]].
