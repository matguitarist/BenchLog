# Getting Started

BenchLog follows every job on your bench, from the moment a device comes in to the day it goes home, and keeps the parts, guides, and invoices that go with it.

## The sidebar

- **Repairs**: every job, by status. The badge counts the open ones.
- **Customers**: the people who bring you devices, with their devices, repairs, and invoices.
- **Devices**: every device, the customers' and the shop's own.
- **Parts**: what you keep on the shelf, with stock, cost, and restock orders. The badge counts parts in low stock.
- **Guides**: repair manuals, as PDFs or Markdown notes.
- **Reports**: what you invoiced and the tax you collected.
- **Log**: what BenchLog did, for when something goes wrong.

The gear at the bottom of the sidebar opens **Settings** (⌘,).

## First steps

1. In **Settings > Business**, enter your business name, address, region, and tax numbers. They print on every document, and the region sets the taxes and the currency.
2. Add a customer with **File > New Customer…** (⌘⇧N), then their device.
3. Open a repair with **File > New Repair…** (⌘N) and print the intake slip for the customer to sign (⌘P).

> [!tip]
> **Search BenchLog** (⌘F) finds any customer, phone number, serial, ticket, note, part, or guide.
