# Backups and Trash

## Backups

A backup is one `.benchlog` file with the whole library: records, photos, files, and guides.

- BenchLog backs up once a day while it is open and keeps the last 14 daily backups.
- **Settings > Backup** shows the folder (Documents > BenchLog Backups unless you choose another), **Back Up Now**, and **Restore from Backup…**.
- Restoring replaces the library with the backup. BenchLog backs up the current library first.

> [!tip]
> Choose a backup folder on another disk or in iCloud Drive, so a lost Mac does not take the backups with it.

## Trash

**Edit > Move to Trash** (⌘⌫) moves a customer, device, repair, or part to the Trash, with what goes with it. **Edit > Undo** (⌘Z) brings it back. **Empty Trash…** in Settings > Backup deletes for good what has been in the Trash over 30 days.
