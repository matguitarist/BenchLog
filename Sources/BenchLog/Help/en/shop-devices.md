# Shop Devices

Some devices belong to the shop: a console you bought to repair and sell, a phone you keep for parts.

1. On the **Devices** page, choose **Add Shop Device…** and enter what you paid.
2. Repairs on a shop device are **internal jobs**: no intake slip, estimate, or invoice, and the last status reads *Done*. Parts installed on them add to the device's cost.
3. Set what became of it: **In Stock**, **For Sale**, **Kept**, or **Sold**.
4. When it sells, enter the price. BenchLog shows the **profit** or **loss**, and **Invoice Sale** invoices it to the buyer with taxes.
