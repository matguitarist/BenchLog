## Tools Needed
- Torx screwdrivers (T10, T15, T20)
- Standard Phillips screwdriver
- Anti-static wrist strap
- Isopropyl alcohol (90%+ concentration)
- Cotton swabs
- Soldering iron and solder (for certain repairs)
- Multimeter
- Thermal paste

## Safety Warnings
- Always unplug the Xbox before opening
- Discharge any static electricity before handling components
- The power supply contains high-voltage capacitors that can hold charge even when unplugged
- Wear appropriate eye protection when cleaning with chemicals

## Xbox Identification
Different Xbox versions (1.0, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.4, 1.5, 1.6) have slightly different internal components. Check the [[xbox-version-identification|Xbox Version Identification]] guide to determine which version you have before beginning repairs.

## Maintenance Recommendations
Even if your Xbox is working properly, consider performing these recommended maintenance procedures:
- Remove the clock capacitor (especially for versions 1.0-1.5)
- Clean the system thoroughly
- Replace thermal paste on CPU and GPU
- Check for early signs of trace corrosion
